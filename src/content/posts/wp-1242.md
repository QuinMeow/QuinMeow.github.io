<blockquote class="wp-block-quote is-style-large"><p>本文方法来源<a href="http://www.zhuangpenglong.com/?p=596" rel="noreferrer noopener">《nextcloud下载超过1G的文件会中断的解决方法》</a></p></blockquote>



<p>Nextcloud在下载超过1GByte文件时，<strong>每下载1GB</strong>都会提示“网络错误”，虽然点击<strong>继续</strong>按钮可以继续下载，但这会大大影响被分享者的使用体验。</p>



<div class="wp-block-image"><figure class="aligncenter size-large"><a href="https://cdn.dalaoweb.top/wp-content/uploads/2020/04/image-17.png"><img src="https://cdn.dalaoweb.top/wp-content/uploads/2020/04/image-17.png" alt="" class="wp-image-1243" loading="lazy" decoding="async"></a><figcaption>Chrome报错</figcaption></figure></div>



<p>实验有效的解决方法：在<strong>Nginx的主配置文</strong>件<code>/usr/local/nginx/conf/nginx.conf</code> 的<code>http{ }</code>下添加如下语句即可</p>



<pre class="wp-block-syntaxhighlighter-code">fastcgi_max_temp_file_size 10240m;</pre>



<div class="wp-block-image"><figure class="aligncenter size-large"><a href="https://cdn.dalaoweb.top/wp-content/uploads/2020/04/image-18.png"><img src="https://cdn.dalaoweb.top/wp-content/uploads/2020/04/image-18.png" alt="" class="wp-image-1244" loading="lazy" decoding="async"></a><figcaption>宝塔如图配置</figcaption></figure></div>



<p>之后重启Nginx或者重载配置即可</p>



<blockquote class="wp-block-quote is-style-default"><p> 估计是客户端下载带宽限制导致服务器php进程fread受限，在php最大执行时间内并没有读完完整文件；fastcgi_max_temp_file_size 则会不受客户端带宽限制，php进程能最大限度的将文件fread完并flush予服务器nginx； </p><cite>原文章评论区留言如是说</cite></blockquote>
