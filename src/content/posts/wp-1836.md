<p>由于Let’s Encrypt两个根证书之一的DST Root CA X3在2021/9/29到期，加上OpenSSL1.0的BUG，导致出现异常的安全连接建立失败。具体原因分析可以参考<a rel="noreferrer noopener" href="https://zhuanlan.zhihu.com/p/417627543">https://zhuanlan.zhihu.com/p/417627543</a></p>



<p>具体到站点上，WordPress表现在更新时提示<strong>“WordPress无法建立到WordPress.org的安全连接”</strong></p>



<figure class="wp-block-image size-full"><a href="https://cdn.dalaoweb.top/wp-content/uploads/2021/11/1635954611-image.png"><img src="https://cdn.dalaoweb.top/wp-content/uploads/2021/11/1635954611-image-edited.png" alt="" class="wp-image-1846" loading="lazy" decoding="async"></a></figure>



<p>Nextcloud表现在“概览”<strong>检查更新</strong>以及<strong>连接商店</strong>时会报错"<strong>cURL error 60: SSL certificate problem: certificate has expired</strong>"</p>



<figure class="wp-block-image size-full"><a href="https://cdn.dalaoweb.top/wp-content/uploads/2021/11/1635948214-image.png"><img src="https://cdn.dalaoweb.top/wp-content/uploads/2021/11/1635948214-image.png" alt="" class="wp-image-1837" loading="lazy" decoding="async"></a></figure>



<blockquote class="wp-block-quote"><p>检查是否能连接时，可以打开“概览”查看是否能检测到更新，“日志”里是否有错误；不推荐打开插件商店，会导致日志被大量写入而无法打开，可以通过后台删除日志清空</p></blockquote>



<hr class="wp-block-separator is-style-wide">



<p>在尝试过网上诸多方法更新证书后，后来检查发现，是PHP编译的OpenSSL版本太低导致</p>



<figure class="wp-block-image size-full"><a href="https://cdn.dalaoweb.top/wp-content/uploads/2021/11/1635948579-image.png"><img src="https://cdn.dalaoweb.top/wp-content/uploads/2021/11/1635948579-image.png" alt="" class="wp-image-1839" loading="lazy" decoding="async"></a></figure>



<p>为了解决这个问题，首先更新证书链，参考<a rel="noreferrer noopener" href="https://www.jibing57.com/2021/10/09/process-expired-let-s-encrypt-ca-in-centos-7/">https://www.jibing57.com/2021/10/09/process-expired-let-s-encrypt-ca-in-centos-7/</a></p>



<pre class="wp-block-syntaxhighlighter-code"># 查看更新
yum list updates -q | grep ca-certificates
# 查看更新日志
rpm -qa --changelog ca-certificates | head -n5
# 安装最新更新
yum -y update ca-certificates
# 检查证书是否还存在
trust list | grep -C3 'DST Root CA X3'
grep -i 'DST Root' /etc/pki/tls/certs/ca-bundle.crt
</pre>



<p>这样操作之后，Bash里使用curl或者wget的SSL连接应该恢复正常。</p>



<p>接下来，需要手动编译安装新版本OpenSSL，参考<a rel="noreferrer noopener" href="https://www.bt.cn/bbs/thread-298-1-1.html">https://www.bt.cn/bbs/thread-298-1-1.html</a></p>



<pre class="wp-block-syntaxhighlighter-code">openssl version

wget https://www.openssl.org/source/openssl-1.1.1l.tar.gz

tar zxvf openssl-1.1.1l.tar.gz

cd openssl-1.1.1l

./config --prefix=/usr/local/openssl

make &amp;&amp; make install</pre>



<p>移动至指定位置</p>



<pre class="wp-block-syntaxhighlighter-code">mv /usr/bin/openssl /usr/bin/openssl.OFF

mv /usr/include/openssl /usr/include/openssl.OFF

ln -s /usr/local/openssl/bin/openssl /usr/bin/openssl

ln -s /usr/local/openssl/include/openssl /usr/include/openssl

echo "/usr/local/openssl/lib">>/etc/ld.so.conf

ldconfig -v</pre>



<p>再检查，发现openssl确实已更新。</p>



<figure class="wp-block-image size-full"><a href="https://cdn.dalaoweb.top/wp-content/uploads/2021/11/1635955076-image.png"><img src="https://cdn.dalaoweb.top/wp-content/uploads/2021/11/1635955076-image-edited.png" alt="" class="wp-image-1849" loading="lazy" decoding="async"></a></figure>



<p>但这时phpinfo显示openssl版本依旧是1.0.2，我们需要在宝塔里<strong>更新</strong>PHP（也可以重新<strong>编译安装</strong>），之后再查看phpinfo就是高版本了。</p>



<figure class="wp-block-image size-full"><a href="https://cdn.dalaoweb.top/wp-content/uploads/2021/11/1635950193-image.png"><img src="https://cdn.dalaoweb.top/wp-content/uploads/2021/11/1635950193-image.png" alt="" class="wp-image-1840" loading="lazy" decoding="async"></a></figure>



<p>至此，PHP的OpenSSL已经升级完毕，站点内通过cURL访问https站点应该恢复正常。</p>
